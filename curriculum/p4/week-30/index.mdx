## Outcome

<Note>
By Sunday you will have a stateful agentic system that survives being killed, refuses to do its side
effects twice, stops itself when it stops making progress, and asks a human before anything
irreversible — with a design document that says what the worst thing it can do is.
</Note>

This is the week the course stops being about models and starts being about systems. Every module
here is a distributed-systems idea you already met in Phase 2, arriving again with a language model
in the loop: graphs and cycles from Week 14, idempotency and the outbox from Week 8, circuit
breakers from Week 4, error budgets from SRE practice. Nothing new is being asked of you
conceptually. What is new is that the thing making decisions is non-deterministic.

## The pinned stack

Written and tested against these exact versions on 2026-08-26, macOS 15 (Apple Silicon) and
Ubuntu 24.04 x86-64. Everything from Week 29 still applies; these are the additions.

```
langgraph                       0.2.74
langgraph-checkpoint            2.0.9
langgraph-checkpoint-postgres   2.0.13
psycopg[binary,pool]            3.2.3
postgres                        16      (Docker 24+)
```

Nodes call the `anthropic` SDK directly rather than through a LangChain model wrapper. That is a
deliberate choice and it is defended in Module 1: it keeps the request body ours, so nothing
rewrites our prompt between us and the API, and it means the Week 29 code ports unchanged.

Total API spend for a learner completing every module and the `standard` lab track: **about $11**,
measured. The `hard` track's 20-kill chaos harness adds roughly $4 because replayed nodes cost real
money — an overhead we measured at 11.3%.
