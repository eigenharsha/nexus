## The story this week tells

You have a model that works. It is also, right now, a brilliant employee with no phone, no
desk, and no ability to work unless you are personally sitting beside it.

This week gives it an address, then everything that follows from having one: serving it without
reloading it on every request, packaging it so it runs somewhere that is not your laptop, and
making deployment a machine's job instead of a Friday-afternoon act of courage.

<Card title="This week — from notebook to product" icon="sparkles">
  - **Give your model a phone number** — an endpoint other software can call
  - **Find the 100× mistake everyone makes once** — loading the model per request
  - **Ship the machine** — containers, layers, and the 6 GB image that becomes 900 MB
  - **Push code, get a deployment** — and a rollback that takes three minutes
</Card>
