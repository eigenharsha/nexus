## The story this week tells

Your container died at 3 a.m. on Sunday. Nobody noticed until Monday, because nothing was
watching and nothing would have restarted it.

This week is the answer to that, and to everything that follows once you have more than one copy
of anything: declaring an outcome instead of giving instructions, a stable name in front of
copies that come and go, limits that stop the platform killing your model mid-request — and the
failure that no infrastructure prevents, where every dashboard stays green while the model
quietly gets worse.

<Card title="This week — operating a model, not just running it" icon="sparkles">
  - **Say “three copies, always”** — and let a system keep it true
  - **Give traffic a stable name** — over parts that keep moving
  - **Find the one-line reason your pod keeps dying** — and set limits you can defend
  - **Catch the failure that never alerts** — drift, and what to do about it
</Card>

`metrics-server` **0.7.2**, `kube-prometheus-stack` **62.3.0** (Prometheus 2.54, Grafana 11.1),
**TensorFlow Serving 2.17.0**, `k6` **0.53.0**, KEDA **2.15.1**, Docker **24.0.7**.

```bash
cat <<'EOF' | kind create cluster --name nexus --config -
kind: Cluster
apiVersion: kind.x-k8s.io/v1alpha4
nodes:
  - role: control-plane
    kubeadmConfigPatches:
      - |
        kind: InitConfiguration
        nodeRegistration:
          kubeletExtraArgs:
            node-labels: "ingress-ready=true"
    extraPortMappings:
      - {containerPort: 80,  hostPort: 8080, protocol: TCP}
      - {containerPort: 443, hostPort: 8443, protocol: TCP}
  - role: worker
  - role: worker
  - role: worker
EOF

kind load docker-image bloodcell:v3 --name nexus     # no registry needed
kubectl get nodes
```

<Warning>
**`kind load docker-image` is the single biggest quality-of-life trick this week.** It copies an
image straight into the cluster's containerd, so you never push to a registry during
development. It also means `imagePullPolicy: IfNotPresent` — with `Always` the kubelet will try
to pull from Docker Hub, fail, and you will spend twenty minutes on `ErrImagePull` for an image
that is already there.
</Warning>
