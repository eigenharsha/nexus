<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I've never written code. Is Week 1 in C really where I should start?">
    Yes, and it's deliberate. Every abstraction you meet later — Python objects, tensors, GPU
    memory, an LLM's KV cache — is a story told on top of memory allocation. Learn the truth once,
    in one week, and the next 31 weeks stop being magic. Layer 1 assumes you have never compiled anything.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have eight years of experience. Can I skip Phase 1?">
    Skim Layer 1, read Layer 2's trade-offs, and go straight to the `hard` lab track. If you pass
    `make verify` on `hard` in under two hours, skip the week. Weeks 8, 11 and 19 are the ones
    experienced engineers most often think they can skip and shouldn't.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a GPU?">
    No. Every GPU lab has a documented CPU or free-Colab path, and Week 26's fine-tuning is sized
    for a free Colab T4 or a 16 GB Mac. You'll also learn to compute the VRAM requirement *before*
    spending money, which is the more valuable skill anyway.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does it cost to run?">
    Under $50 in cloud spend across all 32 weeks. AWS Lambda and API Gateway stay inside the free
    tier, Kubernetes runs locally on `kind`, Postgres and Langfuse are self-hosted in Docker, and
    the LLM work runs locally through Ollama and vLLM wherever it can.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a week missing from the sidebar?">
    Weeks are released as the cohort reaches them. Unreleased weeks exist but are hidden from the
    navigation — see [the release schedule](/start/how-this-works).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are the solutions?">
    Each module and lab links to a hidden solution page. Open it only after you have a failing
    attempt of your own. They're hidden from navigation, not locked — the honour system is the
    only thing between you and wasting your own money.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I found an error, or a Layer 3 that's just Layer 2 in harder words.">
    That's a bug against our own [content spec](/start/three-layers). Open an issue with the page
    URL. Corrections are credited.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
